## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene luxury, Atlantic breeze, and tailored service of a premier 12-key beachfront boutique hotel along the Petite Côte of Senegal. The visual identity sits at the intersection of refined French colonial architecture and warm West African coastal hospitality.

### Visual Aesthetic & Design Movement
- **Atmospheric Coastal Modernism:** Combines architectural structure with organic, maritime relaxation. Clean coastal surfaces pair with rich deep-ocean structural frames and sun-warmed golden touches.
- **Tactile High-Clarity Operations:** Balances an elevated luxury guest-facing aura with an impeccably legible, friction-free tool for boutique hoteliers, front-desk concierges, and operational teams.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet luxury, sea-breeze calm, prestige, effortless control, and immaculate attentiveness.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the Atlantic surf, fine golden sand, and shaded verandas:

- **Primary (`#0e8f9b` - Primary Turquoise):** Represents the coastal water. Used for primary CTAs, active operational states, navigational selection indicators, and interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#e0a53a` - Golden Sand):** Warms the cool maritime palette. Reserved for VIP distinctions, suite tiers, guest ratings, and special concierge highlights.
- **Tertiary / Base Dark (`#0a2540` - Navy Deep):** Evokes ocean depths and stately ironwork. Applied to high-contrast sidebar navigation, authoritative headlines, and structural boundaries.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#eaf1f5` - Coastal Foam):** A soft, eye-soothing cool blue-grey background that reduces ocular fatigue under bright coastal lighting.
- **Surface (`#ffffff`):** Pure elevated card containers with perimeter strokes tinted in `#d8e3ea`.
- **Text Tiers:**
  - *Primary (`#0a2540`):* Highest contrast for room rates, room numbers, and primary headings.
  - *Secondary (`#4a6375`):* Informative context, guest names, dates, and category markers.
  - *Muted (`#7c93a3`):* Metadata, empty states, and field placeholder labels.
- **Operations & Room Status Indicators:**
  - *Libre (Available):* Emerald Green (`#10b981`), surface tint (`#ecfdf5`).
  - *Occupée (Occupied):* Crimson Rose (`#ef4444`), surface tint (`#fef2f2`).
  - *Ménage en cours (Housekeeping):* Warm Amber (`#f59e0b`), surface tint (`#fffbeb`).
  - *Maintenance (Out of Order):* Slate Grey (`#64748b`), surface tint (`#f1f5f9`).

## Typography

The typography pairs an editorial, high-character serif for architectural grace with a balanced geometric sans for operational rigor:

- **Headline Font (Newsreader):** Brings warmth, prestige, and boutique hospitality heritage. Used for property views, room categories (e.g., *Suite Océane*, *Bungalow Baobab*), greeting banners, and daily recap headers.
- **Body & Label Font (Manrope):** Delivers clean readability for operational dashboards, reservation grids, guest folios, and inventory toggles.
- **Financial & Currency Formatting:** All prices follow the regional West African CFA Franc convention (`185 000 FCFA`), typeset with a non-breaking space and fixed-width alignment using `Manrope` semibold or bold.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured operational grid with generous breathing room suited to tablet and desktop front-desk terminals.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop (1200px+): Fixed left rail (260px) in Navy Deep (`#0a2540`) combined with an 8-column flexible content canvas for hotel room cards, calendar matrices, and guest folios.
  - Tablet (768px - 1199px): 4-column canvas with a collapsible rail or top bar, optimized for staff checking rooms on iPad or tablet devices.
  - Mobile (<768px): Single-column view with bottom action sheets for housekeeping and concierge updates on the go.
- **Spacing Scale:** Standard 4px-based rhythm ensuring high tap ergonomics:
  - Outer margins: `margin` (`2rem`) for desktop, adjusting to `1rem` on mobile.
  - Inter-room grid gaps: `gutter` (`1.5rem`) desktop, `gutter-sm` (`1rem`) mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered cool surfaces, soft maritime ambient shadows, and crisp bordering:

- **Surface Stack:**
  - Level 0 (Canvas): Background `#eaf1f5`.
  - Level 1 (Panels & Cards): `#ffffff` with a crisp 1px stroke of `#d8e3ea`.
  - Level 2 (Floating Modals / Guest Folios / Dropdowns): `#ffffff` with a 1px border of `#0e8f9b` (at 15% opacity).
- **Shadow Profile:**
  - *Subtle Card Shadow:* `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.03)`
  - *Floating Elevation:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04)`
  - *Interactive Hover:* Cards elevate on hover by shifting to `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(14, 143, 155, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design balances structured modern cards with friendly, welcoming pill elements:
- **Card Surfaces:** Fixed 16px corner radius (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`), creating an approachable residential silhouette for room grids and operational widgets.
- **Pill Badges:** Complete rounded containment (`9999px`) for room status flags, VIP guest chips, and currency badges.
- **Inputs & Action Elements:** 8px to 10px corner radius for firm, tactile affordance.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Turquoise (`#0e8f9b`), white text, 10px radius, min-height 48px for tactile mobile/tablet use. Subtle shadow on hover (`#0e8f9b` at 25%).
- **Secondary CTA:** Outlined Navy Deep (`#0a2540`) with 1.5px border, transparent background, hovering to `#eaf1f5`.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Text button in Navy Deep with Turquoise hover underline.

### Room Cards (Key Component)
- **Container:** Pure `#ffffff`, 16px border-radius, 1px border `#d8e3ea`, padding `1.25rem`.
- **Header:** Newsreader serif room title (e.g., "Chambre 04 - Alizés"), accompanied by a status pill on the top right.
- **Price Metric:** Large `label-currency` Manrope bold: "185 000 FCFA / nuit".
- **Quick Action Bar:** Contextual buttons for "Attribuer", "Modifier", or "Marquer Propre".

### Status Badges (Pills)
- Fully rounded pills (`padding: 4px 12px`) paired with a 6px circular indicator dot:
  - **Libre:** Background `#ecfdf5`, border `#a7f3d0`, text `#065f46`, dot `#10b981`.
  - **Occupée:** Background `#fef2f2`, border `#fecaca`, text `#991b1b`, dot `#ef4444`.
  - **Ménage en cours:** Background `#fffbeb`, border `#fde68a`, text `#92400e`, dot `#f59e0b`.
  - **Maintenance:** Background `#f1f5f9`, border `#cbd5e1`, text `#334155`, dot `#64748b`.

### VIP & Guest Tags
- Bordered in `#e0a53a` (Golden accent) with a light warm shimmer background (`#fefbf3`), text in `#7c4e0b`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height 48px, background `#ffffff`, border 1.5px `#d8e3ea`, radius 8px, text `#0a2540`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#0e8f9b` with an outer ring in `rgba(14, 143, 155, 0.15)`.

### Operational Matrix / Calendar Grid
- Clean multi-column strip per room with distinct day blocks. Active selections highlighted with a Turquoise fill (`#0e8f9b`) and white text.
